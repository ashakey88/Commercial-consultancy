Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This policy explains what personal data Malleson Labs collects, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. If anything is unclear, get in touch using the contact details below.

1. Who we are

Malleson Labs Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is a commercial consultancy registered in England & Wales. We are the data controller responsible for your personal data when you use this website or get in touch with us.

For any privacy questions, contact us at hello@mallesonlabs.com.

2. The information we collect

We only collect what we need. Depending on how you interact with us, that may include:

  • Email address — when you request access to the Growth Engine demo or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Contact and business details — your name, work email, company/brand name, approximate revenue and the commercial challenge you describe, when you request a free commercial audit.
  • Any information you choose to share — in messages or correspondence with us.

3. How we use your information, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your enquiry or deliver a service you've asked for (e.g. providing the demo link or carrying out a requested audit). Lawful basis: performance of a contract, or taking steps at your request prior to a contract.
  • To send you research, insights and updates by email, where you have asked us to. Lawful basis: your consent.
  • To operate and improve our business and respond to correspondence. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in running and developing the business.

4. Marketing communications

We will only send you marketing emails where you have given us consent to do so — for example by ticking the relevant box or subscribing to our newsletter. You can withdraw consent and unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by emailing us. Requesting the demo or a free audit does not, on its own, sign you up to marketing.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.) — our email and newsletter platform, which stores subscriber data on our behalf.

These providers act under contract and may only use your data for the purposes we specify.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers (including Mailchimp) are based in the United States, which means your data may be transferred outside the UK/EEA. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses to protect your data.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, or as required to meet legal obligations. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Enquiry and audit data is kept for as long as needed to manage our relationship and for a reasonable period afterwards.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email us at hello@mallesonlabs.com.

9. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

10. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
